01 / ABOUT

A little
about me.

I am a Lecturer in the School of Computer Science at Qufu Normal University. I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Dalian Maritime University in June 2026, supervised by Prof. Rongxi He.

My research brings together wireless networking, edge intelligence, and sustainable computing. I investigate how computation, communication, and energy resources can be coordinated in dynamic and resource-constrained networks, with a particular interest in maritime and island IoT.

Before my doctoral studies, I received an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Tianjin University and a B.Eng. in Automation from Inner Mongolia University.
